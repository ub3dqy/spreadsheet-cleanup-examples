# Clear spreadsheets and practical writing

I can help tidy a spreadsheet, write a product description or turn a confusing explanation into a short guide.

## Spreadsheet cleanup and comparison

Remove exact duplicate rows, make unambiguous dates and numbers consistent, and highlight gaps. Or compare two lists and explain the differences.

You receive the finished file, the unchanged source and a short list of changes and questions.

Example quote: **US$15** for an agreed small task with up to 500 rows and 10 columns, covering up to three simple cleanup operations, or two lists with up to 500 rows in total. One correction within the agreed scope is included.

- [Download the cleanup example](01-spreadsheet-cleanup.xlsx): 12 source rows become 10 after removing two exact duplicates. Three uncertain values are kept visible for review.
- [Download the comparison example](02-list-reconciliation.xlsx): two matching product codes and six cases to check. Missing values and real zero quantities remain different.

These are finished examples. Editing the source sheet does not run the cleanup again. Saved data, calculations and sheet layouts were checked; desktop Excel was not run.
